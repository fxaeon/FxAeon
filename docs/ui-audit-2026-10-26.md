# UI audit — 26 October 2026

All 21 full-page captures listed here show the live **PR335 baseline**, before the PR336 polish edits. They are baseline evidence, not screenshots of the candidate. The PR336 source corrections are described separately and still need a fresh capture pass. Values visible in the images are what the live interface displayed at capture time; these screenshots do not establish funded positions or completed transactions.

The comparison was informed by direct browser observations at 390 × 844 on Uniswap, Jumper, Stake DAO, Convex, Aave, and Morpho, recorded in `artifacts/live-polish/reference-study.txt`. These observations are qualitative: prominent amounts with quiet USD equivalents, persistent labels with numeric placeholders during reads, grouped controls, and supporting explanations scoped to their section. They are not speed measurements or claims that those products load faster. FxAeon's own transaction, risk, route, and rate semantics remain authoritative.

## Capture-by-capture findings

Each item distinguishes the visible baseline issue from what PR336 changes or deliberately retains.

## Reference comparison and scope

The reference study supports persistent field labels, numeric placeholders instead of verbose pending sentences, grouped controls, and explanations placed beside the metric or action they clarify. PR336 applies quieter pending-value presentation and reduces repeated captions while retaining accessible labels, rate bases/units, risk disclosures, recipient access, and protocol route context. Borrow funding history keeps the 8-hour period primary; Trade and Portfolio market movement use explicit 24-hour context. Empty Borrow sensitivity should ask for collateral and debt instead of taking space with an empty illustration. Portfolio and Trade desktop columns are retained; Trade's market-card layout is explicitly preserved.
