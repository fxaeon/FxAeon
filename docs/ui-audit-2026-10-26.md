# UI audit — 26 October 2026

All 21 full-page captures listed here show the live **PR335 baseline**, before the PR336 polish edits. Matching candidate captures are stored separately in `artifacts/full-page-audit-candidate/`. Values visible in the images are what the interface displayed at capture time; these screenshots do not establish funded positions or completed transactions.

The comparison was informed by direct browser observations at 390 × 844 on Uniswap, Jumper, Stake DAO, Convex, Aave, and Morpho, recorded in `artifacts/live-polish/reference-study.txt`. These observations are qualitative: prominent amounts with quiet USD equivalents, persistent labels with numeric placeholders during reads, grouped controls, and supporting explanations scoped to their section. They are not speed measurements or claims that those products load faster. FxAeon's own transaction, risk, route, and rate semantics remain authoritative.

## Reference comparison and scope

The reference study supports persistent field labels, numeric placeholders instead of verbose pending sentences, grouped controls, and explanations placed beside the metric or action they clarify. PR336 applies quieter pending-value presentation and reduces repeated captions while retaining accessible labels, rate bases/units, risk disclosures, recipient access, and protocol route context. Borrow funding history keeps the 8-hour period primary; Trade and Portfolio market movement use explicit 24-hour context. Empty Borrow sensitivity should ask for collateral and debt instead of taking space with an empty illustration. Portfolio and Trade desktop columns are retained; Trade's market-card layout is explicitly preserved.

**Baseline:** PR335. **Candidate:** PR336. The original images remain baseline evidence. Candidate phone captures and the mobile menu are recorded separately under `artifacts/full-page-audit-candidate/`; preview environments without production RPC configuration correctly show unavailable on-chain values. These captures establish presentation, not production data availability. No load-time or cross-product speed benchmark was run.

Local verification includes 14 built-route Portfolio/supporting-section checks, seven navigation/menu checks, two follow-up sidebar target/ID checks, 11 corrected source assertions, the Borrow 320px/200%-text help-control regression, lint, app/E2E type checks, and production export/CSP checks. The export remains under the existing 12 MiB static budget after sharing navigation SVG paths. Final-head CI and live release verification remain release gates.

The final export also passes six Borrow/Move supporting-section checks at 320px, 390px, and 1280px, plus three navigation checks covering the 840/1024px rail, wide-screen toggle persistence and target size, and the visually hidden mobile More heading. The compact navigation preserves accessible destination names and a unique controlled navigation ID across retained routes.

## Desktop navigation reference

Jumper was also inspected directly on desktop in its expanded and collapsed states. Its left navigation separates destinations from the upper-right wallet controls, with a utility destination at the bottom. FxAeon adapts that structure using its own six destinations: a 72px icon rail from 840px, an expanded 224px sidebar from 1200px, and a session-persisted collapse control. Mobile retains the hamburger and bottom dock. The hamburger's directory has no visible “More” heading; the heading remains available to assistive technology. Reference captures are `artifacts/live-polish/jumper-desktop-expanded.jpg` and `jumper-desktop-rail.jpg`.

The final desktop Borrow grid continues funding below the overview and sensitivity below the form, with terms beneath both. An extra flexible track absorbs chart height below sensitivity; it must not push sensitivity away from the action. Regression checks require both continuation gaps to stay within 25px without changing the mobile DOM order.
