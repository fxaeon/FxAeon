## Evidence boundaries

`test/fork/results/source-manifest.json` binds the actual native-long local-fork
case to its source/config/vendor inputs. It proves one real protocol lifecycle,
not the other product actions. `docs/evidence/dependency-audit-m4-wallet.json`
records the updated 607-dependency graph's zero-advisory result; audit freshness
and a larger graph do not prove runtime correctness.

The fixture-only `/checkpoint/` page preserves the previous slice's 30 browser
regressions. Production `/app/` now uses the full product UI; it has its own new
browser acceptance. The checkpoint page and synthetic runtime are excluded from
the production build. Synthetic screenshots are labeled Simulation and use
authored example data; supplied reference screenshots are not fixture data.
