# Asset sources

- `tokens/eth.svg`: unmodified Ethereum diamond from [ethereum.org's brand assets](https://ethereum.org/assets), downloaded from `https://ethereum.org/images/assets/svgs/eth-diamond-purple.svg`.
- `chains/base.svg`: the existing repository Base square (`apps/mini-app/public/wallets/base.svg`), consistent with [Base's current identifiers](https://brand.base.org/core-identifiers). Keep its proportions and corner geometry; do not turn it into a circular icon.

These marks identify the supported assets and network. They do not imply endorsement of FxAeon.

`brand-film.mp4`, `brand-film-mobile.mp4` and `brand-film-poster.jpg` are original FxAeon artwork, rendered from the source in `../artwork/`. The scene uses procedural geometry and lighting, without downloaded models, textures or stock footage. Three.js (MIT) and GSAP are offline authoring dependencies; neither is included in the landing runtime.
