# FxAeon — Current

Original abstract motion artwork for the public landing page. Two continuous, broad metal ribbons exchange places in a studio: polished silver and cobalt enamel. Large curved surfaces carry soft-box reflections. The movement has weight, a slow turn and a clear resting frame. No interface, numbers, charts, coins, words, particles, glow, or simulated transactions.

Palette comes from the landing contract: cobalt #2459e8, warm neutral #f5f4ef, dark neutral #171917. Silver is a material reflecting the same environment, not another brand accent. No typography appears in the film.

One uninterrupted seven-second scene. The camera stays steady; the sculpture turns and the light travels over it. Frame for a square master with generous upper/lower crop room, retaining the two crossing surfaces on a phone. The website plays it once when visible, provides play/pause/replay, and stays on the still for reduced motion, data saving, missing JavaScript, or media failure.

This film is decorative. It conveys material, movement, and care, without representing returns, security, transactions, or protocol mechanics.
