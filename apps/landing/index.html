What is FxAeon?

FxAeon is an interface for f(x) Protocol on the web and in Telegram.

Does FxAeon hold my funds?

No. Your wallet approves each transaction. FxAeon does not receive or store your private keys.

Which networks can I use?

Trade, Earn, and Borrow run on Ethereum. Move bridges fxUSD and fxSAVE between Ethereum and Base.

How do savings and withdrawals work?

Deposit fxUSD or USDC into fxSAVE, f(x) Protocol’s savings vault. Its APY is variable. Withdraw instantly for a fee, or queue a redemption and claim after the cooldown shown in the app.

What does it cost?

Review estimated gas and network costs when data is available, plus applicable protocol fees, before signing. Bridge transfers include a LayerZero fee quote.

What should I know about risk?

Leveraged positions and borrowing can be rebalanced or liquidated. Savings yields are variable and returns are not guaranteed. Smart contracts, tokens, wallets, bridges, and networks carry risk. Read the f(x) Protocol docs before you start.
